This follows The Colab's own For Good research method — every task asks for:
  1. Executive answer — the direct answer, 1–2 sentences
  2. Evidence — 2–3 sourced facts, not vibes
  3. Confidence — High / Medium / Low, honestly rated
  4. What would change this — what info would flip the conclusion
Same shape as a real finding in research/findings/ on the For Good repo — just compressed into a 5–10 minute phone task.

Grounded in thecolab-ai/.skills — 100+ keyless, open NZ public-data tools (fuel prices, DOC huts, libraries, grants, GeoNet, and more) built by The Colab community. Your AI doesn't need the skill installed — it just needs to find the same kind of public data any way it can, and cite it.
